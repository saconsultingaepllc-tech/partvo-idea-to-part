# Measurements and fit

Ask for the dimension that resolves the next uncertainty, not a complete intake form. Identify endpoints in plain language: outside edges, hole centers, reference face, or pin axis. Distinguish total spacing changes from moving each side. Preserve dimensions already measured or fit-tested.

For photographs, request a ruler or caliper in the plane of the feature with both endpoints visible. Use overall views for topology and close/side views for mating geometry. Perspective and obscured endpoints limit accuracy; pixel counts do not establish manufacturing tolerances. Label surfaces consistently across images, the brief, and the model.

Prioritize mating diameters, center spacing, insertion depth, wall thickness, fastener access, and motion envelopes. Establish offsets or diagonals before assuming symmetry. For hollow parts, define the opening direction, outer cross-section, wall/web thickness, and depth independently.

Separate a bearing surface from its connector: reinforcing the root must preserve axis location, engagement length, free ends, and clearance. Assess movement against measured mating geometry or a known clearance envelope. Otherwise mark full travel as awaiting a physical test.

Choose fit-test coupons that preserve the constraints being tested. Record revision/hash, absolute parameter values, material/process, orientation, and acceptance criteria. Give variants durable labels or a documented layout recoverable from photos. Diagnose insertion, motion, strength, and print-quality failures separately before requesting a revision from Astra.
